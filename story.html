✦Her story

From the spine to the spirit

Every part of Dr. Leslie's work traces back to her own body: a spine that failed early, a healing that followed, and a practice that kept deepening until it reached the level of faith itself.

✦

Fourteen

A steel rod at fourteen

A severe childhood spinal disorder led to spinal-fusion surgery at fourteen: multiple vertebral joints fused with a steel rod, followed by months in a body cast. By her early twenties she was living with sciatica and chronic low-back pain.

It was chiropractic care that finally brought relief. That experience, suffering first, then a way out, became the spark for the rest of her life's work.

✦

The doctor

The chiropractor's calling

She trained as a Doctor of Chiropractic at Life University in the Atlanta metro, then built a practice helping others out of the kind of pain she knew firsthand.

Over more than two decades, she noticed that spinal and health patterns tended to recur. That observation pulled her from structural care toward the nervous system, the core causes beneath the symptoms, and the energetic dimension of healing.

✦

2012

The download

In 2012, while working on patients, she experienced what she describes as a spontaneous download: the beginning of the teaching she now calls Faith Consciousness, an understanding of human consciousness moving through three eras, rooted in scripture, revealed through science, realized in the individual.

The teaching itself lives at faithconsciousness.com. This site is where to bring her, and it, to your community.

✦

2025

The testimony

In 2025, Dr. Leslie was diagnosed with breast cancer. She kept it largely private. What happened next is hers to tell, and she tells it below.

✦Her account

A spontaneous healing

By embodying the principles of Faith Consciousness, alongside specific tools and lifestyle changes, she experienced what she calls a spontaneous healing. She describes the result as proof that she now carries "the formula of change" in her own field, applicable to health, to relationships, and to finances alike.

This is Dr. Leslie's account of her own experience. Her first-person telling will appear here, verbatim, once she supplies it. It is her testimony, not medical advice, and no medical claims are made on her behalf.

✦Beyond the practice

Wells for Wells

Her outreach has reached as far as Uganda. By her account, her Wells for Wells initiative brought fresh running water for the first time to the Vine School near Masaka in 2016, and in 2012 she served as emcee of a Global Women's Summit in Uganda, the same season the teaching began.
