✦The experience

What an event can include

Every gathering is shaped with the host. These are the elements she draws on.

The teaching

An accessible introduction to Faith Consciousness: the three eras, and the shift from life happening to me, to for me, to through me. Roughly ninety minutes in its full form.

Guided SRI breathwork

Somato Respiratory Integration: the gentle, guided breath practice she pairs with the teaching, done seated, no experience needed.

Light-language activation

A short experiential activation she offers where the setting welcomes it, as at her Awaken the Formula gatherings.

Community elements

At some venues, a drumming circle closes the gathering. For health fairs, her presence scales down to a table, a talk slot, or both.

✦Hosting

How it works

  1. Tell her about your event

    The inquiry form asks for your venue, dates, audience, and what you have in mind. Two minutes, one organized place.

  2. Shape it together

    A short conversation settles the format: full gathering, talk slot, table presence, or something in between.

  3. She shows up ready

    Dr. Leslie arrives with everything the format needs, and your community gets the experience you booked.
